## Methodology, from the research passes

**EMEA:** Searched EU-Startups.com, Tech.eu, TechCrunch, Sifted.eu, Crunchbase News, and regional press (Wamda, RAED Ventures, Vestbee, FinSMEs) for seed/Series A announcements from roughly May through August 2026, cross-referenced each company against Tracxn/LinkedIn/company career pages for team size, and searched separately for founder names plus "CTO"/"LinkedIn"/"GitHub" to find named technical contacts. Direct fetches of EU-Startups.com articles were blocked (403), so results relied on search-result snippets and syndication sites (Vestbee, FinSMEs, Tech.eu, BeBeez) quoting the same press releases, cross-checked against at least two independent sources per funding figure where possible. Dropped: Zerolook (Zug, Switzerland, pre-seed) for no confirmable engineering stack signal, and a UK-founded decentralized AI data network for being a Web3 "strategic round" from crypto-native investors rather than a standard seed/Series A. Team-size figures sourced from Tracxn/company-data aggregators are noted as such since they aren't always independently press-confirmed.

**APAC:** Searched DealStreetAsia, e27.co, TechCrunch, Inc42, YourStory, TechNode Global, DataCenterDynamics, and Tracxn/Crunchbase/PitchBook aggregator pages for "seed"/"Series A" crossed with sector keywords (cloud, DevOps, Kubernetes, infrastructure, backend, data platform) and 2026 dates, including DealStreetAsia's weekly "Editor's Take" roundups to scan multiple deals at once. Cross-checked team size and named technical contacts against LinkedIn, Tracxn, and Crunchbase person profiles before including. Dropped: Qashier (Singapore, real Series A+, but ~140-150 employees per LinkedIn/PitchBook/RocketReach, too large), LinqAlpha ($22M Series A but HQ'd in New York, not APAC), Archestra.AI (funded, exhibited at KubeCon India, but UK-based), Coredge (India, Kubernetes/edge cloud, but no 2026 funding round found), Optimaze and Kimia (Australia, real seed rounds, but Optimaze's round was October 2025/too old and Kimia's stack signal was weak). Japan and Korea searches mostly surfaced Series B+ or non-English-first companies, which is why neither appears above rather than being force-fit.
